🌽 Traditional Brazilian Pamonha 🌽

Prep time: 30 mins
Cook time: 40 mins
Yields: 6 servings

Ingredients

    6 large cob of corn
    1 cup granulated sugar (adjust to taste)
    ½ cup whole milk or coconut milk
    3 tablespoons unsalted butter
    A pinch of salt
    Corn husks (carefully removed and washed)

Instructions

  1. Prepare the husks: Peel the corn carefully to keep the outer husks intact. Wash them thoroughly in hot water and set aside to dry.
  2. Cut the kernels: Using a sharp knife, slice the corn kernels off the cobs. Avoid cutting too close to the cob to prevent bitter bits.
  3. Blend the mixture: Place the corn kernels, sugar, milk, melted butter, and a pinch of salt into a blender. Blend until smooth. (You can leave it slightly textured if you prefer more bite).
  4. Form the pouches: Take two corn husks and overlap them slightly to form a small cone or cylinder shape. Fold up the bottom edge tightly to create a sealed pocket.
  5. Fill and tie: Pour about ½ cup of the corn batter into the husk pouch. Fold down the top edges of the husks and securely tie the middle with a piece of string so water cannot leak in.
  6. Cook: Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil. Carefully drop the pamonhas into the boiling water. Cover and let them cook for about 40 minutes until firm.
  7. Serve: Remove them carefully with tongs, let them cool slightly, cut the strings, peel back the husks, and enjoy warm or at room temperature!
Tip: If you prefer the savory version (pamonha salgada), substitute the sugar with salt, and feel free to add small cubes of fresh cheese or minced cooked sausage inside the pouch before tying!
